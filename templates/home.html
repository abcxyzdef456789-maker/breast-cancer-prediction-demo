THE IMPORTANT CONTEXT

A good score is only the beginning.

This classroom project uses five features from one dataset and one train/test split. Real health technology needs extensive validation on diverse, independent populations, clinical oversight, and regulatory review. A benchmark score is not evidence this model is safe or appropriate for real-world medical use.
